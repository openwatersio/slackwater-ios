# Siri and Shortcuts

Slackwater includes three App Shortcuts: Next Low Tide, Next High Tide, and Next Slack Window. Installing the app makes them available to Siri, Spotlight, and the Shortcuts app. A separate shortcut download is unnecessary.

Open Slackwater and allow location before asking for a nearby prediction. Try "When is the next high tide in Slackwater?", "When is the next low tide in Slackwater?", or "When is the next slack window in Slackwater?" The actions also appear under App Shortcuts → Slackwater in Shortcuts.

## Place and date parameters

Each action has optional Place and Date parameters in Shortcuts. With both left empty, it answers the next event near the person, as above. Place searches the bundled names, regions and aliases, with separate tide and current choices. For example, save Savannah as a favorite, then try "When is high tide at Savannah in Slackwater?" For a matching spoken name, selection follows the person's station list: My Location first (including a chosen namesake), then favorites in their saved order, then recents in most-recent-first order. My Location is used only with a recent authorized fix; naming a place never starts a location request. All candidates must match the requested text and prediction type. A matching list entry resolves directly. Otherwise, candidates rank by exact name, partial name, region or alias, then distance from a recent fix, with name/region/id as deterministic ties. Remaining ambiguous catalog matches stay available for Siri to disambiguate; the app does not force an arbitrary catalog choice. An explicitly selected place never falls back to another place or asks for the person's location. Unavailable saved predictions produce a named error directing the person to open Slackwater.

Siri's named-place trigger phrases use at most 100 suggested places per prediction type, in My Location, favorite, then recent order. They do not expand across the world catalog. [Apple limits an app to 1,000 expanded trigger phrases](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10102/?time=1373), including entity substitutions; the two tide phrases and one current phrase share that budget. Suggestions use identity metadata without loading prediction records. Slackwater refreshes the published parameters at launch and when the scene changes phase, so returning from the app after saving a favorite makes that place eligible for Siri. A place outside these suggestions remains searchable in the Shortcuts Place picker, where a saved shortcut can explicitly select it. Direct spoken recognition of every catalog name is not supported by this bounded phrase registration.

Date selects a whole calendar day at the chosen place. The selected calendar date is retained when the phone and place have different time zones; a local day can span 23 or 25 hours. High/low actions list every matching tide on that day, including times earlier today. The slack-window action lists windows overlapping the day, including windows that cross midnight, without inventing unobserved boundaries. Dated answers speak the date and exact local times rather than a relative wait. The curve is anchored at the requested day's midnight with its usual look-back and highlights its first matching tide or window.

App Shortcut invocation phrases include Place. Apple's App Shortcut phrase substitutions do not support an arbitrary Date, so Date is exposed as an intent parameter rather than interpolated into a phrase. Configure it in Shortcuts, including through an Ask Each Time variable for Siri to collect when running a saved shortcut. Recognition and date collection for an unconfigured sentence such as "high tide tomorrow in Savannah" require device validation and are not established by the parameter metadata or unit tests.

## Place selection

High and low tide requests use the nearest available tide prediction. Slack-window requests use the nearest available current prediction. A recent location fix lets the action answer directly in Siri. When that fix needs refreshing, the action uses App Intents' dynamic foreground continuation before requesting location, with a five-second bound on that request. If foreground continuation is unavailable, or location is denied or unavailable, Siri asks the person to open Slackwater and allow location. A favorite, recent place, or first-run coordinate does not substitute for the person's location.

Predictions use the same bundled and saved models as widgets. A Canadian place that needs a downloaded prediction must have one saved before Siri can use it. Online current predictions must cover the widget loader's full required span. Siri skips a provisional current fit until the place's complete model is saved, so the card does not present its provisional speeds without their warning. A timing-only derived gate cannot answer a request for a measured current window. Selection has no distance cap, so the nearest available prediction can be far away. Each prediction names its place. If the selected current place has data but no slack window in the next 48 hours, Siri reports that at the named place without trying a farther one. With the comfort current Off there is no window anywhere, and Siri says the window is off and how to turn it on.

## Answers

Speech names the place and gives an approximate elapsed wait, rounded to whole hours when at least an hour remains and whole minutes below an hour. For example: "The next high tide at Oak Bay is in about six hours." Heights and full dates stay out of speech. An upcoming slack window includes its duration; an open window gives the time remaining, including after the slack instant has passed. Window boundaries use the comfort-current setting shared with the app and widgets.

Duration and remaining-time claims require an observed closing crossing. If a current remains below the comfort setting at the sampled end, Siri omits the ending time: "The slack window at Deception Pass is open now." A clipped sampling boundary never appears as the window's opening or closing. Speech describes the query time; the reused widget's live countdown can continue to advance while the result remains visible.

The visual result leads with the same relative answer Siri speaks. The exact details appear beneath it in smaller text, followed by the shared wide widget content. Tide results include the local event time and height in the selected units. Current results include the observed local window boundaries. Speech and the current card use the same sampled curve, including its reading and countdown. The curve highlights the requested tide or window, even when another event occurs first, and includes the requested event when it falls beyond the widget's usual visible span. When a window has an unobserved boundary, the curve marks its known opening or the current moment instead of showing a complete range.

## Validation

`TideShortcutTests` checks the expanded-phrase budget, suggested-place ordering and deduplication, relative answers, tide selection, observed versus clipped window boundaries, provisional-model selection, foreground preparation for stale location, and agreement between spoken and visual results. Rendered snippet attachments cover tides and currents at compact widths, for manual inspection of larger answer and detail text above the fixed-size widget drawing. The tests do not cover Core Location failure callbacks or the five-second timeout. Tests that exercise App Intents in-process do not prove that Siri recognizes a spoken phrase or presents the snippet on a physical device.

Device verification covers named-place recognition, same-name disambiguation, configured and Ask Each Time dates, cross-time-zone dates, all three original phrases, Siri with the app closed, location denied or unavailable, a current window before and after slack, and speech alongside the visible result. Website examples should use a verified device capture.
