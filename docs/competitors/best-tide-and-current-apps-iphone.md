# The best tide and current apps for iPhone

Every one of these apps will tell you the next high tide. They differ on the harder
questions: does it work with no signal, does it predict the current in a pass, does it
cover Canada, and what does it cost after the first week.

We make one of them. The comparison is still straight, because the people reading this
own boats and kayaks and will check.

## What to look for

- **Currents, not just heights.** A tide app that shows current speed as one number is not
  a currents app. You want the slack time, how long it lasts, and max flood and ebb with a
  direction.
- **Offline predictions, not an offline cache.** Tides are astronomy. An app that computes
  them on the phone works in the anchorage; an app that caches yesterday's fetch works until
  it doesn't.
- **Named data.** NOAA or the Canadian Hydrographic Service, by name, per station. "Trusted
  providers worldwide" is not a name.
- **Both sides of the border,** if you are anywhere near it.
- **What is actually paid.** Several free tiers end at a date range, an ad, or a three-hour
  window.

## Slackwater

Free, no ads, no account. Tides worldwide, currents across the US and Canada, all
computed on the phone from NOAA and CHS data. A current station shows the next slack and
how long it lasts, the speed and set at max flood and max ebb, and a curve you can scrub
through the week. The chart draws offline too.

![Slackwater: Nakwakto Rapids scrubbed to 5:40am, slack at 0.5 kn, flood 12 minutes later, with the 10.3 kn ebb and 5.2 kn flood marked on the curve and green slack runs drawn on the line](/shots/compare/slackwater-nakwakto-slack.png)

Nakwakto Rapids at slack: 0.5 kn, flood 12 minutes later, with the 10.3 kn ebb and 5.2 kn flood either side. The green runs on the line are the slack windows.

What it lacks: a Watch app, alerts, weather. It is in public beta on TestFlight and needs
iOS 26. If you want a tide app on your wrist today, it is not this one yet.

**Best for:** boaters and paddlers who need the slack answer with no bars, on either side
of the border.

## AyeTides XL

The one boaters keep. $7.99 once, since about 2010, no subscription, fully offline, and it
has real current stations, 12,500 locations worldwide. BC pilots use it. The interface is
from another era and the current answer is a table you read rather than a window you are
shown. Paddlers report BC current stations are thin and a few sit on land.

[Read the full comparison](/compare/slackwater-vs-ayetides/).

**Best for:** current stations outside North America, in one purchase; anyone who trusts the XTide tables.

## Tide Alert (NOAA)

The alerts app. Tell it a tide level or a time and it notifies you, on the phone or the
Watch. Now covers Canada, Mexico and Latin America, and favourite stations work offline.
Currents are shown as speed over the tide chart with estimated slack windows, which is
useful but is not a current station prediction. Full access is $19.99 a year, or $99.99
for life.

[Read the full comparison](/compare/slackwater-vs-tide-alert/).

**Best for:** anyone who wants the phone to say "the tide is at 2 ft now" without opening it.

## Tide Guide

The best-looking tide app on the platform, with an Apple Design Award to prove it. Full
Watch app, Live Activities, a dozen marine weather conditions beside the tide, 15
languages. Currents are one number in a data box, no stations, no slack. Widgets and
complications need Pro. Reviewers outside the US report datum and timing errors, and the
app does not say where its data comes from.

[Read the full comparison](/alternatives/tide-guide/).

**Best for:** beach and surf conditions at a glance, on a Watch.

## Tides Near Me

Open it and it shows the nearest station, rising or falling. That single interaction earned
161K ratings. It is free with ads and has no offline claim. Currents are direction and timing, no speed curve.

[Read the full comparison](/alternatives/tides-near-me/).

**Best for:** "is it coming in or going out" on a beach with signal.

## Actual Currents

Currents-first, and the best picture of flow you can get on a phone: animated particles
over a nautical chart, with a time scrubber and route analysis. Canadian stations arrived
this summer. It streams everything, so it needs a connection, and the free tier is a
three-hour window around now. No tides, no widgets, no Watch. Launched April 2026.

[Read the full comparison](/compare/slackwater-vs-actual-currents/).

**Best for:** seeing how a strait moves at a chosen hour, from the dock.

## Tides and Currents-USA

Offline harmonic tides and currents for the US, with 849 harmonic current stations, for
$19.99 a year. Reviews complain about the station picker. US only.

**Best for:** US-only offline currents on a budget, if you can live with the interface.

Each app above has its own page with a side-by-side screenshot and the full table:

- **Transiting passes in BC or Washington:** Slackwater. Second choice AyeTides.
- **Alerts on your wrist:** Tide Alert.
- **Weather and a Watch face:** Tide Guide.
- **A picture of the whole strait moving:** Actual Currents, with signal.
- **Just rising or falling, free:** Tides Near Me, with ads.

Predictions are not observations. Weather, river flow and local conditions change the
water. None of these apps, including ours, is for navigation.
