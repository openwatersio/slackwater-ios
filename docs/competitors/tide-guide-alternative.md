# A Tide Guide alternative for people who need the current

Tide Guide won an Apple Design Award and deserved it. It has the best Watch app in the
category, Live Activities that start themselves at a chosen time, and a dozen marine
weather conditions beside the tide. If that is what you want, stop reading and keep it.

People look for an alternative for a few specific reasons.

- **Currents are one number.** Current speed lives in a data box you have to reselect
  every time. There are no current stations, no slack times, no max flood or ebb.
- **The subscription.** Widgets, complications and the detailed charts are Pro: $29.99 a
  year, $49.99 one-time, or $150 lifetime. For tide math that never changes.
- **Accuracy outside the US.** Reviewers report wrong datums and times on the UK coast,
  and the app does not say where its data comes from.
- **Weight.** 320 MB and an always-on location warning in the listing.

## Slackwater, in short

Free, no ads, no account. Tides worldwide, currents across the US and Canada, every
prediction computed on the phone from named NOAA and Canadian Hydrographic Service data.
Home screen widgets are free. It is a public beta on TestFlight, iOS 26 and later.

It has no Watch app, no weather, no Live Activities. Those are Tide Guide's strengths and
they are real.

## Where the difference is

**The current is the harder half.** Heights tell you how much water there is. The current
tells you whether you can get through the pass. Slackwater's current detail leads with
the state now, the next slack and how long it lasts, and max flood and ebb with speed and
direction, over a curve you scrub through the week. The reference port's tide is on the
same screen. Tide Guide has nothing equivalent.

**Named data, checked.** Every Slackwater station says NOAA or CHS and which datum the
heights sit on. The engine is validated against the agencies' own predictions and the
deviations are published. Tide Guide's provenance is a phrase.

**Free widgets.** Tide Guide reviewers say the widgets alone justify the subscription.
Slackwater's home screen widgets show the next slack or tide turn and the day's curve,
for nothing. There is no server behind them, so there is nothing to charge for.

**Offline as the default.** Predictions on the device, chart on the device.

## Who should switch

Switch if you run a pass, paddle in current, or want your tide app to keep working past the
last cell tower without a subscription.

Stay with Tide Guide if you want the Watch, the weather bar, or Live Activities. Slackwater
does not have them and is not pretending otherwise.

## Moving over

Nothing transfers. Search your stations by name and star them; nearest ones are already
sorted to the top. A few minutes for a long list.

Predictions are not observations. Weather, river flow and local conditions change the
water. Slackwater is not for navigation.
