# XTide for iOS — the free-and-clean incumbent, stale and US-only

*2026-08-21. Found by Bryan via a [r/boating PSA (Dec 2023)](https://www.reddit.com/r/boating/comments/18s70qz/tide_app_ios_best_free_one_xtide/) recommending it because every other iOS tide app is "littered with ads or need to pay." Snapshot of the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/xtide/id1191127595) (CA storefront).*

Lee Ann Rucker's revival of the ~2005 "TideApp" port of XTide. This is the closest existing
occupant of Slackwater's *positioning* (free, no ads, no account, offline, open-data
harmonics) — and it demonstrates the niche is real but undefended:

- **Free**, no IAP; collects zero user data. iPhone/iPad/Watch, widgets + complications;
  offline once station data loads.
- **US-only harmonics** "due to legal data restrictions" — i.e. the exact CHS wall our
  licence architecture (`chs-data-model.md` §3) gets through. No Canadian stations unless
  the user sources a custom harmonics file themselves.
- **Stale:** v2.0.3, March 2023 — no update in ~3.5 years. Too few ratings for the App Store
  to even show an average, despite the market-research table showing its XTide-lineage
  cousins (AyeTides 4.8★/~1.3K at $7.99) thriving on the same data.
- Hobby-grade presentation ("NOT FOR NAVIGATION", no warranty) with no currents emphasis,
  no slack windows, no gauge comparison.

Read with `market-research-2026-07-17.md` (AyeTides/SEAiq rows — same XTide lineage,
paid tiers): the free tier of this lineage is abandoned, the paid tier is US-current-sparse
in BC. Slackwater's free-core + US-and-Canada + currents-first combination remains
unoccupied. Also a warm door for GTM #6 (XTide crowd): Rucker kept the open-data faith
alive on iOS; worth a friendly note someday, not a competitor to flank.
