# MyTide.ie — Irish indie tide PWA

*2026-08-21. Found by Bryan via the maker's [launch post in r/DublinSeaSwimming](https://www.reddit.com/r/DublinSeaSwimming/comments/1srprv1/new_tide_app_for_irish_tides/) (Apr 2026). Snapshot of [mytide.ie](https://mytide.ie/) at v2026.7.20.*

Not a market competitor — Ireland-only coverage, zero overlap with US/Canada. Interesting as
a **convergent design**: two brothers independently built almost exactly slackwater-web's
shape, for the same reasons ("fed up with overcomplicated ones").

## What it is

- Free web app + installable PWA (manifest + service worker; "Install App" is a first-class
  nav item), no ads, no account, Stripe tip jar for server costs.
- Data: Marine Institute — Irish National Tide Gauge Network + MI harmonic tide predictions,
  ~2 years of free predictions. Heights on **LAT**, local Irish time.
- Views: daily / monthly / table, sunrise/sunset/moon phase, spring & neap ranges for the
  year, OSM location map, per-location URLs (`mytide.ie/Sligo`).
- "Not intended for navigational purposes" disclaimer + a proper predicted-vs-real-time
  explainer on the About page.

## Two ideas worth stealing

1. **Tide gauge vs. modelled overlay** — the chart plots the live gauge trace against the
   prediction. That's our accuracy-receipts argument (GTM #4) as a *UI element*: every user
   sees the prediction earn trust against reality, continuously, instead of reading a
   validation writeup. Only works where a live gauge exists, which is exactly the
   station-class data we already know how to reason about.
2. **Places-of-interest with safe crossing windows** — Coney Island (tidal island, drivable
   at low tide) gets a card with crossing windows. That's the transit-window/gate concept
   packaged for pedestrians. The audience asymmetry is the lesson: swimmers/walkers get
   windows ("when can I cross"), not curves — same shape as our slack-window framing for
   boaters.

## Audience note

Their stated users: swimmers, surfers, anglers, walkers, photographers — boaters last. Same
beyond-boaters demand the GTM #8 thread ledger documents (`sailingnaturali/slackwater` →
`docs/gtm.md`). Their launch pattern (genuine post in a niche sub, warm reception, feature
requests in replies) is the playbook proof for that channel.
