1.14.0 (54)

This Beta collects the changes since 1.13.0:

· A rebuilt station map with new symbols, previews, and more reliable navigation from map pins and shared links.
· More resilient downloads, clearer progress, and explanations for unavailable stations.
· Updated tide and current station databases, with faster loading and more reliable saved station choices.
· Home-screen widgets have live gallery previews, improved timeline loading, and fixes for station links.
· French Canadian and Spanish translations, locale-aware readings and clocks, and clearer station and location prompts.
· Accessibility improvements for larger text, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and tap targets.
· Solar eclipses alongside lunar eclipses, clearer Moon information, and distinct symbols for current and sun events.
· Fixes for stale readings after reopening the app, edge-swipe navigation, iPad date pickers, and search.
· An in-app link to report problems with a station, tide, or location.

Worth testing: Choose and search for stations, download predictions for offline use, and reopen the app after time away. Check home-screen widgets and links to stations. Try French or Spanish, larger text, and VoiceOver. Report any readings, station names, or navigation that seem wrong.
