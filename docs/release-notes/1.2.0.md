1.2.0 (25)

The chart leaves the Salish Sea. Stations have been national since 1.1.0, but the
chart under them stopped at home water — open San Francisco and you got bare land
with nothing on screen saying why. Now there are seamarks and shaded depths
anywhere in the US and Canada, coarser than home water and drawn under it.

· The current strip colours by actual speed. Every curve used to be fitted to its
  own extremes, so a 3-knot pass and Sechelt Rapids drew the same picture; now the
  colour says which one you're looking at.
· Four more gates, 22 in all — the first on the Atlantic (Great Bras d'Or) and the
  first on Haida Gwaii (Masset Sound). Nakwakto and Masset need a signal; the other
  two work offline.
· Gates outside the Pacific now show their slacks in their own time zone. Great
  Bras d'Or would have been four hours out.
· Pending cards say what they're doing with an icon and two words instead of two
  lines of prose, and the fast answer names its own tolerance — "Refining · ±35 min"
  — where a warning triangle used to sit.
· 28 stations that could only ever show "Failed" are gone: they advertise
  predictions and serve none. Five Great Lakes and St. Lawrence stations became
  usable in the same pass.
· Station names no longer repeat their own region — no more "Tecumseh Ontario · ON".

Worth a look: open a station well outside BC — Puget Sound, San Francisco, Boston —
and see what the chart draws. Then open Sechelt Rapids next to somewhere quiet and
compare the strip colours.
