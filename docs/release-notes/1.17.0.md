1.17.0

Current windows show more of the times the water is moving comfortably, and tide predictions cover more places.

· Current windows now include periods when the current slows below your comfort setting without stopping completely. True slack remains distinct from a comfortable current.
· Canadian online current stations keep their measured speed when the direction is unknown, instead of treating it as zero. Older downloads can still show an approximate reading while updated predictions download.
· Tide predictions improve at some stations, and the catalog adds gauges including Anchorage and places in Greenland. The two Maizuru gauges are shown separately.
· Update paired devices together. Newly refitted Canadian current models need the updated app on the paired device. Some older one-time current-window alerts may ask you to review their selection.

Worth testing: open a current station whose flow slows without stopping and check its comfort windows. Open a Canadian online current station with and without downloaded predictions. Check an existing one-time current-window alert, and update a paired device before testing a newly refitted Canadian current model.
