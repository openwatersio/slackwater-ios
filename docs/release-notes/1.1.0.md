1.1.0 (24)

The same app as the 0.7.0 build you never saw: TestFlight wouldn't offer it, because
0.7.0 sorts below the 1.0 that builds 1–21 shipped by accident. This one is numbered
above it.

· The schedule hangs from a date you pick, not from today — a range bar heads the card
  and the date picker moves the window.
· Online-only gates (Sechelt Rapids and the rest) fetch a month at a time and prefetch
  when you open the picker; a week with no data says so instead of going blank.
· The offline chart labels itself — station names and cluster counts render with no signal.
· CHS stations take their colour from what's cached on the device, instead of drawing
  grey in our home waters.
· Days end at midnight, including the two a year that are 23 or 25 hours long.
· The strip hints that it scrolls by moving, instead of telling you to swipe.

Worth a look: pick a date a few weeks out and scrub, then open the map in airplane mode
to see the labels.
