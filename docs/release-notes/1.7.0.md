This release makes Slackwater quicker to use from the map, widgets, and system shortcuts.

· Current stations now animate speed and direction directly on the map, including an approximation through Dodd Narrows.
· Tide widgets can follow your current location and show a clearer miniature tide curve.
· App shortcuts take you straight to tide stations and the nearest slack-water timing.
· Station downloads and live CHS waits show clearer progress, while favourites load sooner across devices.
