This release makes slackwater timing easier to read at a glance.

· Set the current speed you are comfortable with in Settings. The chart, slack windows, and map use that same value.
· Current charts now show the selected slack threshold, a fixed speed scale, peak-to-peak range, and a clearer green slack window.
· Tide charts keep their blue water, add bold curve-following chevrons where the tide moves quickly, and explain fast and extreme movement when you land on it.
· Scrubbing is clearer: the reading floats with the curve, the chart has a fixed left-hand scale, and a subtle Now button returns you when you have moved away from the present.
· Current terminology now leads with Incoming or Outgoing, followed by Flooding or Ebbing.

Worth testing: try a low and high slack threshold in Settings on a familiar pass, then scrub to a fast-moving tide segment and back to Now. Tell us whether the threshold and motion cues match what you would expect on the water.
