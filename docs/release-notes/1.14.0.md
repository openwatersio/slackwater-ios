Welcome to the first release of Slackwater, the tides & currents app that works offline where you need it — on the water, at the beach, in the anchorage. View past, present, and future predictions at thousands of stations worldwide, already on your phone.

· Tide and current predictions on a timeline you can scrub, with sun, Moon, and eclipse events alongside.
· A station map and search, and nearby tide and current stations on every station page.
· Canadian tides and currents from CHS, downloaded for offline use.
· Home-screen widgets for the next event and today's tide curve.
· An Apple Watch app with tide and current complications, scrubbed with the Digital Crown.
· Calendar and notification alerts for slack windows, tides, and eclipses.
· Ask Siri for the next high tide, low tide, or slack window.
· Available in 14 languages.
· Slackwater Premium is an optional purchase; the offline core is free and stays free.

Worth testing: open the Premium purchase sheet and check that it shows the subscription term and links the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Add Next Event and Today's Curve from the home-screen gallery, choose a station for each, and tap each widget to confirm it opens its own station. On an Apple Watch, add a complication and scrub a place with the Digital Crown. Set an alert for a slack window and check that it arrives.
