1.14.0

A map that says what the water is doing, downloads that survive a bad connection, and a way to tell us when a reading looks wrong.

· The map draws what each station is reading. A current is an arrow pointing where the water flows, coloured by speed; a tide is a gauge filled to the height's place in its cycle, rising or falling. Zoom in and each pin carries its number. The basemap is a dark vector chart, and tapping a pin previews the station over the map, one tap from the full page.
· Stations we know about but may not publish numbers for now appear as hollow rings instead of empty water, and tapping one says so and offers the nearest places that do have predictions.
· Canadian downloads keep going on a slow or interrupted link. The Downloads screen shows queue order, per-station progress, time estimates and when a retry will happen, and station cards carry the same progress in the list.
· Concerns or Feedback sits in every detail footer. Pick what looks wrong and it opens a mail with the station, the moment on screen and a link back to it already filled in.
· Solar eclipses join lunar ones: on the timeline, in the schedule, as scrub stops, and on the Moon tile and sheet with how much of the sun is covered.
· The station list opens on My Location, with Downloads and Settings in a footer below the groups.
· Fixes: a detail left open in the background catches up to the clock when you return; a shared link's moment lands on the station it names and nowhere else; the falling-rate text stays readable against a sunrise sky; the iPad date picker no longer hides weeks; an edge swipe on the station list no longer freezes navigation; a widget pointing at a station that is no longer in the app explains itself; the Range, Moon and Almanac cards fit their content.

Worth testing: open the map in a busy harbour and at continent zoom, tap pins of both kinds, and check the preview opens the right station. Find a hollow ring in Europe and follow it to the nearby stations. Start a Canadian download on a weak connection, interrupt it, and watch the queue and the retry timing recover. Send a report from a detail after scrubbing to tomorrow and check the mail carries that moment. Leave a detail open, background the app for half an hour, and see whether the reading catches up.
