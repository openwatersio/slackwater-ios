Welcome to the first release of Slackwater, the tides & currents app that works offline where you need it — on the water, at the beach, in the anchorage. View past, present, and future predictions at thousands of stations worldwide, already on your phone.

Worth testing: this build goes to App Review, so the checks a simulator cannot answer are the ones that matter. Add Next Event and Today's Curve from the home-screen gallery, choose a station for each, and confirm the picker applies. Check that no Slackwater widget appears in the lock-screen picker, and that neither Settings nor the Widgets gallery mentions Premium. Open a detail, swipe back from the edge, then edge-swipe on the station list and confirm stations still open. Tap a home-screen widget and confirm it opens its own station.
