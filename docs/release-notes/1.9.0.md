Station cards, the detail scrubber and the medium widget now share one chart: a day of the curve with the past faded, so the reading sits on the shape it came from.

· Every tide and current card in the list draws about a day of its curve behind the reading — one swing of history, three ahead — with dots and values at the turns, times along the bottom, and a marker riding the curve at now. Current cards turn green for each usable slack window.
· The detail scrubber takes the same look on both tracks: values hang off the peaks and turns with no unit, the slack run is drawn on the line with dots at its opening and closing, the tide's rate of rise is carried by line colour instead of chevrons, and night and day bands run full height. The readout shows the set beside the speed.
· The medium widget renders the card, with a "SLACK ENDS" countdown once under two hours remain.
· Two Canadian ports added (Cap-des-Rosiers, Rivière-Madeleine); Dodd Narrows now reads "Northumberland Channel".
· Fixes: the Now button works while the scrubber is still gliding; the derived gate (Malibu Rapids) shows the floating Now once you scrub away; an online gate with nothing downloaded no longer offers a week picker that leads nowhere; on-device fits anchor to the station's day rather than the phone's time zone; the slack readout prints the threshold the strip was drawn with; the station list and online-gate scrubber stop redoing work on every frame; day labels sit at true noon across daylight-saving changes.

Worth testing: scan the list and see whether the card graphs tell you what you'd have tapped in for. Flick the detail scrubber hard and hit Now before it stops. Add the medium widget and compare it against the same station's card. If your phone's time zone isn't Pacific, download a CHS station and check its fit window ends on yesterday's date at the station.
