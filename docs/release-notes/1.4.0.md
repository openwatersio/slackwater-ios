1.4.0 (28)

Slackwater is on your home screen now. Add the small widget for the next slack or
tide turn at your station, or the medium one for today's whole curve — both work
offline, both free, no account. The 3-second test: station, curve, next event,
answered before the app opens.

· Deep links from the widgets. Tap either one and it opens straight to that
  station, or to a page explaining every widget family if it can't resolve one.
· A Widgets page in Settings walks through what's free and what's Premium, with
  instructions for adding each — home screen widgets are a long-press away,
  lock screen ones live under Customize.
· Slackwater Premium is new, and it's quiet about it. Three lock screen widgets —
  next slack above the clock, next event as a circular glance, the workable
  window as a rectangular glance — for a yearly subscription or a pay-once
  lifetime unlock. Everything you use today, including both home screen widgets,
  stays free forever. Premium is how development gets funded; buy it because you
  want it, or because you want to say thanks.

Worth a look: add both home screen widgets for a station whose curve actually
moves today, and check the time against the app itself — the widget reads the
same fitted model, just rendered smaller.
