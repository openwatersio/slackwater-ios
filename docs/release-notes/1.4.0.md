1.4.0 (28)

Slackwater is on your home screen now, and the map says more about what the water
is doing. Everything here is free, works offline, and needs no account.

· Widgets. The small one gives you the next slack or tide turn at your station;
  the medium one draws today's whole curve. The 3-second test — station, curve,
  next event — answered before the app opens. Tap either and it opens straight to
  that station.
· A Widgets page in Settings walks through each family and how to add it: home
  screen widgets are a long-press away, lock screen ones live under Customize.
· Pins carry state as colour now. Current stations are circles coloured by speed —
  near-black when the stream is barely running, brightening through red to gold as
  it builds — and they turn green exactly when the station is inside its slack
  window. Green means transitable. Tide stations are squares: blue when the tide
  is rising, orange when it's falling.
· Tide detail reads the rate of rise — ft/hr or m/hr — beside Rising/Falling,
  along with the range to the next extreme. The tide track is coloured by that
  rate, so a 12 ft/hr run and a lazy harbour tide no longer draw the same picture.
· Every chart window now includes the past 48 hours, whichever day you're anchored
  on, and paging back on an online gate is a disk read rather than a refetch.

Worth a look: put both home screen widgets on a station whose curve actually moves
today and check them against the app — the widget reads the same fitted model, just
rendered smaller. On the map, find a narrows around its turn and watch the pin go
green, then compare it against the slack window on the station's own card.

One thing to know about the pins: green is a decision, not a magnitude. A station
at 0.4 kn is green and one at 0.6 kn is nearly black, because the threshold is the
slack window itself rather than a point on the colour ramp. If that reads wrong on
the water, say so — it's the call we most want tested.

Not in this build: the three lock screen widgets are visible in the Widgets page
but aren't purchasable yet, so they'll show as locked if you add one. Nothing you
use today is affected — the home screen widgets and everything else stay free.
