1.2.1 (26)

Small release on top of 1.2.0. The one that matters: a favorite whose station left
the app used to disappear without a word — the row vanished, the section header with
it if that was your only favorite, and the app never said anything had happened. 28
stations left in 1.2.0, so this was live.

· A favorite that is no longer shipped keeps its slot and says so, named as you knew
  it. Remove it, or pick a replacement near where the old station was.
· Cards lost the little wave and dome in front of the name. They taught a new reader
  nothing and a returning one reads the name — where it still matters, the card says
  "Tide" or "Current" in words.
· "Sable Island/Sable, Île de" reads properly. It came out of the DFO feed with the
  French half mangled, the only bilingual name in 1086 stations.

Worth a look: if you favorited a station that has since disappeared on you, it should
be back in the list with a way out.
