Ask for an alert in your own words, plan around the golden and blue hour, and keep your place on iPhone Duo.

· Type or dictate when you want an alert, such as "an hour before tomorrow's low" or "whenever it rises past 8 ft", and Slackwater fills in the alert for you to check. Apple's on-device model reads the sentence, so nothing leaves your phone. This needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
· Every tide and current timeline has a line for setting an alert. Press and hold the moment you care about, then tap the line. One sheet makes and edits every alert.
· Alerts can be limited to daylight, the golden hour, or the blue hour, and can follow only the lower low or higher high of each day. Alerts are part of Slackwater Premium.
· A Sun card sits beside the Moon with the next sunrise or sunset and the day's daylight. The Sun sheet lists golden hour and blue hour, and tapping any time in it moves the timeline there.
· The Moon is drawn tilted the way it looks from the station.
· Folding or unfolding an iPhone Duo keeps the moment, the week, and your place in the schedule.
· Slackwater is available in Irish and Scottish Gaelic.
· New Zealand tides use harmonic constants from Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand.
· When a station's record changes, your favorites, alerts, widgets, and watch complications follow it. A station Slackwater no longer carries says so and suggests nearby replacements.
· Canadian downloads carry on in the background when you reopen the app. On online current stations, the timeline moves on into the next week and downloads it. Downloads shows whether you're online, and each online current station has one control for its expiry and updates.
· The timeline stops where your finger lifts, unless an event is within 15 minutes.
· A Help link in Settings opens Slackwater's support page.
· Current timelines tell slack from the slack window. The line is yellow where the water runs faster than the slack window you set in Settings and blue inside the window, where the reading keeps saying Flooding or Ebbing. Only slack itself, under 0.15 kn, is green and reads Slack.
· Fixes: tapping an alert notification no longer crashes Slackwater when it has been in the background; the widget, Siri, and the station card agree on the next tide at places where the tide is very small.
