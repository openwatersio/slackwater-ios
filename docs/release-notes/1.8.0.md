1.8.0 (33)

The map goes offline in this release: chart tiles now download to the device instead of shipping inside the app.

· The map renders a satellite basemap that keeps working with no signal. Three tiers download on their own — the world, the area around your location, and 20 km around every starred or downloaded station — and gaining signal no longer swaps the chart out from under the marks. The app itself is about 109 MB smaller for it.
· Current stations show a static direction arrow instead of the 1.7.0 animation, and the map toggle that went with it is gone.
· Downloads can fetch every Canadian current gate in one go — 13 gates, about 25 minutes — so a passage planned from the dock has its gates fitted before you are near them.
· The map opens on your location immediately at launch, and station detail gives the map at least a third of the screen.
· Fixes: the charts card no longer flashes "Some map areas didn't finish" while packs are being created, finished packs stop being re-downloaded on a loop, tide widget labels fit better, and the slack timing readout holds steady. Settings links the privacy policy.

Worth testing: let the map packs finish, then turn on airplane mode and see how far you can pan and zoom before the chart runs out. Compare a current station's arrow against its station detail. If you are outside the Salish Sea, try the currents download in the Downloads screen and tell us whether the gates you actually care about came with it.
