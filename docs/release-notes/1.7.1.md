1.7.1 (32)

Fixes on top of 1.7.0, led by a crash that hit while offline data was downloading.

· The app no longer freezes and quits partway through a station download. Every redraw of the Downloads screen was re-reading and re-decoding the whole online-data file, which starved the app until iOS killed it.
· Tapping a tide widget opens that station instead of dumping you on the station list — including when the widget is added from the gallery before the app has ever been opened.
· Downloads now lists only the stations near you and reports an honest total. From outside the Salish Sea the nearest-station picks could reach hundreds of kilometres away, and nine rapid-current gates sat in the list that could never finish.

Worth testing: start a few station downloads and leave the Downloads screen open while they run, then add a tide widget and tap it. If you are away from the Pacific Northwest, check that what Downloads offers you is actually nearby.
