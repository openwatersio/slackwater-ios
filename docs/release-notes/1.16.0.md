Canadian downloads say how long they last and keep themselves a month ahead, and Premium adds control over the sky.

· Online current stations in Canada, such as Nakwakto Rapids, show the last day their downloaded predictions cover, as "Through Nov 26", and refresh once a day whenever you're online, so that date keeps moving ahead. The station page keeps this in its own Offline available card, apart from the navigation notice. Station data is always free.
· Turning off the sun, moon and stars is part of Slackwater Premium. If you turned them off before, they stay off. With them off, the timeline also leaves out sunrise and sunset.
· Setting Slackwater up on another device skips the first-run tour if you have seen it on one of your devices. Replaying the tour from Settings turns the sun, moon and stars back on for it.
· An alert on the timeline is a line with a bell.
· Nearby and the station chooser show full station cards, with readings and curves, so you can compare stations before opening one.
· Seventy Japanese tide gauges use harmonic constants published by the Japan Meteorological Agency.
· If you share favorites between devices, update them together. An older app can show a migrated favorite as removed; deleting that card also removes the favorite from your updated devices. Older builds cannot schedule alerts for migrated gauges after you return to them.
· Fixes: the Dodd Narrows map arrow points along the published ebb bearing; favorites, recents, and alerts stay with a station whose gauge Slackwater still carries under another record.

Worth testing: online, open Sechelt Rapids or Nakwakto Rapids and check the Offline available card reads "Through" a date about three weeks ahead; open it again the next day and check the date has moved. Downloads should say station data is always free. Without Premium, try turning off the sun, moon and stars in Settings and check the Premium sheet opens; with Premium, turn them off and check sunrise and sunset leave the timeline and the commentary. Finish the tour on one device, then install on a second device signed in to the same Apple ID; the tour should not start. Set an alert and check its line and bell on the timeline.
