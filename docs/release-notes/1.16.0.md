1.16.0

Alerts count down on the Lock Screen, current timelines mark the strongest flow, and Canadian downloads say how long they last.

· An alert's last half hour, and a slack window while it is open, count down in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. The alert notification starts the countdown, so nothing buzzes twice.
· Current timelines show max ebb and flood times in a row below the slack times.
· Online current stations in Canada, such as Nakwakto Rapids, show the last day their downloaded predictions cover, as "Through Nov 26", and refresh once a day whenever you're online, so that date keeps moving ahead. The station page keeps this in its own Offline available card, apart from the navigation notice. Station data is always free.
· A Premium setting turns off the sun, moon and stars above the chart, along with sunrise and sunset on the timeline. The sky keeps its day and night colors, and the daylight tint under the curve is stronger.
· Each moment in the Sun sheet has an info button that explains it, such as how far below the horizon the Sun is at astronomical dawn.
· Setting Slackwater up on another device skips the first-run tour if you have seen it on one of your devices. Replaying the tour from Settings turns the sun, moon and stars back on for it.
· An alert on the timeline is a line with a bell.
· Nearby and the station chooser show full station cards, with readings and curves, so you can compare stations before opening one.
· The map shows its tide and current units once, in a legend that opens Settings.
· Seventy Japanese tide gauges use harmonic constants published by the Japan Meteorological Agency.
· If you share favorites between devices, update them together. An older app can show a migrated favorite as removed; deleting that card also removes the favorite from your updated devices. Older builds cannot schedule alerts for migrated gauges after you return to them.
· Fixes: the Dodd Narrows map arrow points along the published ebb bearing; favorites, recents, and alerts stay with a station whose gauge Slackwater still carries under another record; the sky no longer turns amber where the tide stays below chart datum; the slack timing note under a Canadian current stays readable at large text sizes.

Worth testing: set an alert for a few minutes ahead, lock the phone, and check the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with one buzz. Open a current station and check the max ebb and flood row under the slack times. Online, open Sechelt Rapids or Nakwakto Rapids and check the Offline available card reads "Through" a date about three weeks ahead; open it again the next day and check the date has moved. Downloads should say station data is always free. Without Premium, try turning off the sun, moon and stars in Settings and check the Premium sheet opens; with Premium, turn them off and check sunrise and sunset leave the timeline and the commentary. If you turned them off in an earlier beta, they should still be off without Premium. Finish the tour on one device, then install on a second device signed in to the same Apple ID; the tour should not start. Set an alert and check its line and bell on the timeline. Compare a few stations in Nearby, and tap the map's units legend.
