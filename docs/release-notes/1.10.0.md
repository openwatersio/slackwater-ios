1.10.0 (35)

More stations, a redesigned detail view, and shared links that open the station at the moment you chose.

· Added subordinate tide and current stations.
· Station details put the graph up front, with a new header, readout tiles, distance from your location, and animated skies powered by Almanac.
· Shared station links open the matching station and selected time in Slackwater.
· Station cards use the detail graph's drawing rules, with clearer tide fills and slack windows. Pending cards show loading placeholders.
· The small widget shows the current reading over its station's curve. Widget memory use has been reduced to avoid hitting the extension's limit.

Worth testing: open a shared station link and check both the station and selected time. Compare a subordinate station with its reference station. Scrub through day and night in the redesigned detail view, check the distance with location enabled, and try the small widget on your Home Screen.
