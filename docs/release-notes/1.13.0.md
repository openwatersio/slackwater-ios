1.13.0 (38)

Find nearby places from any detail page, and see how the Moon can shape the tides.

· Each station page now lists nearby tide and current stations with a map, so you can move between places without returning to search.
· The Moon sheet explains spring and neap tides and how the Moon's distance can affect tidal range. Phase shortcuts take you to the relevant night sky.
· Search shows where same-name stations are and remembers the place you selected. Results use more space on iPad.
· First launch offers ready Chesapeake Bay stations and a clearer location choice.
· Canadian tide and current downloads recover better from slow or interrupted connections, and downloaded currents appear in widgets.
· The Now button returns the schedule to the current week. Current pages show station details consistently.

Worth testing: open a station and use its nearby list and map to switch places. Compare two same-name search results, then reopen your choice. Open the Moon sheet around a full or quarter moon. Start a Canadian download on a weak connection, interrupt it, and check that it resumes; then add its current to a widget.
